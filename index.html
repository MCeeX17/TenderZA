About TenderZA

Still in production. TenderZA is being built and tested. Expect changes, missing features and the occasional bug. No contract is awarded through this site.

TenderZA connects South African organisations that need goods and services with businesses that can supply them. Public and private sector organisations post what they need, with quantities, budget limit and dates. Businesses browse, price each item, say where they sourced it, and apply.

You can save a quotation and come back later while you gather prices. After you apply, TenderZA tells you which certified documents to prepare. You only send them once the organisation contacts you.

TenderZA does not choose winners, verify companies or replace official government portals such as the CSD. Always check the tender's own rules.

Privacy

Still in production. This policy is a working draft and will be reviewed against POPIA before launch.

What we collect

Your account details (name, company, phone, email), the tenders you post, and the quotations and drafts you save or send. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.

Who can see it

Tenders are visible to logged-in users. A submitted quotation, including your contact details, is visible only to you and the organisation that posted that tender. Drafts are visible only to you.

Documents

TenderZA does not collect or store your certified company documents. You send them directly to the tendering organisation after they contact you.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore). We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the site is used.

Your choices

You can delete your saved drafts and submitted bids in My bids, and organisations can delete their tenders. To remove your account or ask about your data, contact the site owner (contact email to be added before launch).
